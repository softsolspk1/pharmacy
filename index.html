About the Faculty

The cradle of pharmacy education in Pakistan

The Faculty grew out of the Department of Pharmacy, founded in 1964 to meet the country's need for hospital and retail pharmacists and to serve Karachi's pharmaceutical industry, home to more than 90 percent of the nation's drug manufacturers.

In 1973 the department became the first full faculty of pharmacy in Pakistan, with four teaching and research departments. Today it has five departments, purpose-built academic blocks, the Research Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences (RIPS) and a bioequivalence facility that serves industry in Pakistan and abroad. More than 13,000 graduates work in academia, industry, hospitals, community pharmacy and regulatory bodies around the world.

  • Revised NCRC-HEC/PCP Pharm.D. curriculum, implemented from 2026
  • Capstone projects and an Experiential Learning Program (ELP)
  • MoUs with tertiary-care hospitals for clinical clerkships
  • Two scholarly journals, including the PJPS (Impact Factor 0.692)

Undergraduate Program

Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D.)

A five-year professional degree following the NCRC-HEC curriculum revised by the Pharmacy Council of Pakistan. It combines classroom teaching, extensive laboratory work, capstone projects and hospital clerkships.

Practical training

Experiential learning & clinical clerkships

Final-year students complete a Pharmacy Practice Experience (PPE) clinical clerkship at affiliated tertiary-care hospitals. Under the revised curriculum, students also take on capstone projects mentored by faculty with links to industry, hospitals and community pharmacy.

Bridging program

Pharm.D. (Deficiency) Program

For holders of the four-year B.Pharm. degree who want equivalence with the five-year Pharm.D. It consists of 18 courses (43 credit hours) over two semesters.

Admissions & Regulations

Admissions, rules & student honours

Admission criteria, attendance and promotion rules, gold medals and convocation guidelines.

Merit-based selection

Admission is strictly on merit and requires a competitive pre-admission entry test (minimum score 50%). Applicants are placed in one of three categories:

  • Category K: passed the prerequisite exam from the Board of Intermediate Education Karachi or the University of Karachi.
  • Category S: passed intermediate exams from other boards in Sindh.
  • Category P: qualified from other provinces of Pakistan.

Foreign students (20 seats)

Twenty foreign students are admitted to the Pharm.D. program each year.

The Faculty does not accept these applications directly. They must be processed and endorsed by the applicant's embassy and the Ministry of Economic Affairs, Islamabad.

Seats are also reserved for persons with disabilities and for children of university employees, and are awarded on competitive merit.

80% promotion rule

  • Students who pass at least 80% of their courses in an academic year are promoted to the next professional class.
  • There are no special examinations for the 1st to 4th professional years. A failed course is repeated with the regular class.
  • A supplementary examination is held only for the 5th professional year, six weeks after results are announced.

Attendance & degree duration

  • Students need 75% attendance in each subject to sit university examinations.
  • The Dean may condone a shortfall of up to 5% on valid grounds, and the Vice Chancellor a further 10% in cases of special hardship. Students below 60% may not sit the exams.
  • The Pharm.D. must be completed within 7 years. Absence for 3 consecutive weeks cancels the admission.

Academic regalia & ceremony

  • Invitation card: required for entry with an authorised parent or guardian.
  • Regalia: the official academic gown, cap and hood.
  • Faculty colour: the hood colour for the Faculty of Pharmacy is grey.
  • Tassel: wear it on the right before your degree is conferred, then move it to the left to show you have graduated.
  • Decorum: arrive on time and keep silence during the formal proceedings. Once all degrees are conferred, graduates toss their caps together.
